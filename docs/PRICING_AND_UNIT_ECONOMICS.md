# Pricing & Unit Economics

Launch pricing remains a configurable hypothesis, not a market fact.

- Micro: ₦1,750/month in the included demo configuration.
- Small: ₦3,500/month in the included demo configuration.
- Trust Events and enhanced verification: configurable usage prices.
- Direct-cost target: approximately 25–30% of selling price as a design target, subject to actual measured provider/infrastructure/support/fraud costs.

Cost ledger categories should include provider, compute, storage, messaging, support, fraud review, payment collection, refunds/credits, taxes/fees and fallback delta.

KYC/KYB/AML are separate cost objects. Do not require expensive checks on every low-risk transaction merely because an integration can perform them.
