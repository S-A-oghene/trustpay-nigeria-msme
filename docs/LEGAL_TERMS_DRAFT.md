# DRAFT — REQUIRES NIGERIAN LEGAL/COMPLIANCE REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION

## Service boundary draft

TrustPay provides evidence, transaction-control and business-control functionality. It does not itself take custody of customer funds in the base product. Payment services are delivered by separately licensed providers where enabled.

## Trust verification limitation

TrustPay displays evidence and verification results within the scope of the configured checks. A verified identity, account or transaction is not a guarantee of future conduct, product quality or absence of all fraud.

## Unknown / unavailable

Where authoritative evidence is unavailable, TrustPay may display UNKNOWN or require additional verification. It should not infer fraud from missing evidence alone.

## Disputes

A dispute record is not itself a finding of wrongdoing. Outcomes depend on the evidence available and the authority granted to the relevant reviewer/provider.

## Privacy

TrustPay should collect only information necessary for the stated purpose, protect restricted information, retain it only as long as justified, and provide applicable user rights and disclosures under reviewed Nigerian law.
